---
name: No campaign chatbot
description: Standing product decision against chatbot features on the campaign website.
---

Do not add a chatbot to this campaign website unless the user explicitly requests it again.

**Why:** The user requested complete removal after abusive political messages and said the campaign does not need the headache.

**How to apply:** Keep chat widgets, launch buttons, and third-party chat loaders out of all pages. Existing CRM forms and campaign phone contact are not chat and should remain functional.
